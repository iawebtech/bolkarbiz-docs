# Use cases — Bolkar BIZ as a business OS

Pick the story closest to your shop. You still install one app; you change **business type**, catalogue, and roles.

---

## 1. Kirana / general retail

**OS job:** Fast counter + stock that does not go negative without you knowing.

**Typical day**

- Morning: glance low-stock, receive a box of biscuits (stock in).
- Day: barcode or search billing; UPI and cash mixed.
- Evening: today’s total vs cash drawer; note credit khata customers.

**Setup emphasis:** products + GST if registered + cashier role. Skip bookings.

**Devices:** Windows or Linux at the counter; Android in the aisle for “is this in stock?”

---

## 2. Salon / spa / clinic-style services

**OS job:** Chair time + product add-ons (oil, kit) on the same bill.

**Typical day**

- Book walk-in or WhatsApp appointment.
- Staff starts the service; add retail products to the same ticket.
- Close bill; next booking reminder.

**Setup emphasis:** services with duration, staff as resources, customers with phone numbers.

**Devices:** iPad / Android at reception; phone with the stylist for the next slot.

---

## 3. Restaurant / cafe / sweet shop

**OS job:** Table or parcel tickets, kitchen qty, day-part sales.

**Typical day**

- Punch items (plate, kg, combo).
- Parcel vs dine-in as notes or categories.
- Close shift: item-wise (what ran out) not only rupees.

**Setup emphasis:** categories (beverages, snacks), modifiers as separate items if you do not use complex BOM yet.

**Devices:** Windows/Linux counter; Android for owner peeking from home.

---

## 4. Repair / field service

**OS job:** Job card → parts used → invoice.

**Typical day**

- Create customer + complaint.
- Book slot or take walk-in job.
- Consume spare parts from inventory onto the job bill.
- Collect; print job invoice.

**Setup emphasis:** services + spare-part products + customer history.

**Devices:** Technician Android; desktop in the workshop.

---

## 5. Multi-counter / small chain

**OS job:** One catalogue, several tills, one owner report.

**Rules that matter**

- Same shop login / invited staff, not five separate shops (unless they are legally separate).
- Prefer **online** during business hours so stock does not double-sell.
- Owner desktop for catalogue; cashiers locked to POS.

**Devices:** One Windows/Linux per counter; owner macOS or Windows for reports.

---

## 6. Tier-2 / weak network

**OS job:** Never stop billing because the fibre died.

**Practice**

- Train cashiers: “no internet is normal.”
- Do not switch devices mid-bill.
- When the line is back, wait for sync before comparing two tills.

Voice AI for Hindi (and other supported languages) is aimed at operators who should not hunt through menus.

---

## 7. Owner who is not at the shop

**OS job:** See today, approve nothing dangerous from a phone.

- Install iOS/Android.
- Role = owner.
- Check today’s sales and dues; do not give the helper owner login.

---

## What Bolkar BIZ is not (yet) a replacement for

- Full statutory accounting suites (Tally-class year-close) — export/report, then your CA.
- Marketplace storefronts (Amazon/Flipkart) — this is **your** counter OS.
- Consumer WhatsApp for personal chat — only optional business messaging you connect.

If your workflow is “counter + stock + people + simple books,” you are in scope.
